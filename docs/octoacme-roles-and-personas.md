# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Quality Assurance / Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads own the quality strategy for projects, ensuring all features meet acceptance criteria and quality standards before release. They define testing approaches, manage test planning, and coordinate QA efforts across delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Define testing strategy (unit, integration, end-to-end, security scanning)
- Create and maintain test plans aligned to acceptance criteria
- Coordinate manual and automated testing efforts
- Identify quality gaps and blockers; escalate critical issues
- Collaborate with developers on test design and coverage
- Manage QA sign-off gates before release
- Capture and track defects; prioritize bug fixes

### Goals
- Ensure high quality and reliability of delivered features
- Reduce defects escaping to production
- Maintain clear quality metrics and test coverage

### Typical Communication
- Participation in sprint planning and definition of acceptance criteria review
- Daily standups and QA status updates
- Quality reviews and sign-off communications

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **With Developers:** Partners on test design, automation framework, and defect prioritization
- **With Product Managers:** Validates acceptance criteria and defines quality standards for acceptance
- **With Project Managers:** Reports on quality metrics and escalates blocking QA issues; coordinates release readiness gates

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters facilitate agile ceremonies and coach teams on process adherence. They remove impediments, promote retrospective learnings, and help teams continuously improve their delivery practices.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate daily standups, sprint planning, and retrospectives
- Shield team from external distractions and escalate blockers
- Coach team on agile practices and continuous improvement
- Track and follow up on retrospective action items
- Maintain project visibility and remove process bottlenecks
- Collaborate with PM on sprint planning and backlog refinement

### Goals
- Maximize team velocity and efficiency
- Foster continuous improvement culture
- Ensure consistent process adherence

### Typical Communication
- Facilitation of all ceremonies and team meetings
- Escalation of blockers and process impediments
- Retrospective follow-up and action item tracking

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **With Project Managers:** Supports planning ceremonies and escalates process-level blockers for cross-team resolution
- **With Developers:** Facilitates standups and removes impediments; coaches on agile technical practices
- **With Product Managers:** Ensures backlog refinement is well-structured and sprint planning remains efficient

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads provide technical strategy and architectural guidance for projects. They make critical technical decisions, review design approaches, and ensure technical excellence and maintainability.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical architecture and design approaches
- Conduct technical design reviews and code reviews
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigations
- Mentor developers and coach on technical best practices
- Ensure scalability, security, and maintainability standards
- Collaborate with product on technical feasibility and trade-offs
- Guide integration and dependency management across teams

### Goals
- Deliver technically sound, scalable solutions
- Reduce technical debt and rework
- Foster technical excellence across the team

### Typical Communication
- Technical design discussions and architecture reviews
- Code review feedback and mentoring
- Technical risk assessments during planning and execution

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **With Developers:** Provides architectural guidance, mentorship, and code review feedback to ensure quality and scalability
- **With Product Managers:** Advises on technical feasibility, trade-offs, and implementation timelines
- **With Project Managers:** Identifies technical risks and dependencies; escalates architectural blockers affecting the schedule

---

## Stakeholder / Executive Sponsor

### Role Summary
Sponsors provide executive oversight, funding authority, and business alignment for projects. They champion initiatives, remove organizational blockers, and ensure alignment with business strategy.

### Responsibilities
- Provide project sponsorship and funding authority
- Champion project within the organization
- Ensure alignment with business strategy and priorities
- Participate in decision gates and key milestone reviews
- Remove organizational barriers and escalate as needed
- Approve scope changes and budget adjustments
- Provide stakeholder feedback and validation

### Goals
- Ensure business value delivery and strategic alignment
- Enable rapid decision-making and unblocking
- Maintain stakeholder engagement and support

### Typical Communication
- Monthly stakeholder updates and milestone reviews
- Decision gate participation
- Ad-hoc escalation and approval meetings

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **With Project Managers:** Reviews project status, approves scope changes, and provides strategic direction; escalates organizational blockers
- **With Product Managers:** Validates business priorities and success metrics; ensures alignment with organizational strategy
- **With Developers/Technical Leads:** Participates in decision gates and provides context on business constraints and priorities

---

## Security / Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Officers ensure that projects meet security standards, compliance requirements, and risk management policies. They review security approaches, manage compliance activities, and coordinate incident response.

### Responsibilities
- Define security and compliance requirements for projects
- Conduct security reviews and threat assessments
- Ensure security scanning and testing is integrated in CI/CD
- Manage compliance audits and regulatory requirements
- Coordinate incident response and post-incident analysis
- Review release plans for security and compliance readiness
- Provide security guidance and training to teams

### Goals
- Minimize security vulnerabilities and compliance risks
- Ensure regulatory and organizational standards are met
- Enable rapid, secure incident response

### Typical Communication
- Security reviews during planning and pre-release
- Incident response coordination and communication
- Compliance and audit participation

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **With Developers:** Provides security guidance, reviews code for vulnerabilities, and integrates security testing in CI/CD
- **With QA/Testing Lead:** Collaborates on security test strategy and penetration testing coordination
- **With Project Managers:** Reviews release plans for security readiness; escalates compliance issues and security risks
- **With Technical Leads:** Advises on secure architecture and design patterns; participates in threat assessments
